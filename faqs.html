Frequently Asked Questions

Getting Started

Answers for prospective deer farmers

Starting a deer operation is a significant decision. These common questions provide a practical first look at what to consider.

01

Why should I start a deer farm?

Deer farming can be a rewarding way to combine a passion for wildlife with agriculture.

02

Is the deer farming industry growing?

Yes. Deer farming continues to be an important and growing part of the agricultural economy, supported by breeders, hunting preserves, and related businesses across the United States.

03

How much land do I need to start?

The amount of land depends on your goals and stocking rate, but it can require considerably less land than crop or other livestock operations. Many farms start with 5–20 acres.

04

What type of fencing is required?

In Kansas, an 8-foot-high fence is the industry standard. Good fencing protects your investment and helps keep your herd safe.

05

How much does it cost to get started?

Startup costs vary greatly based on your goals and operation size. Land, fencing, handling facilities, breeding stock, feed, and equipment all affect the initial investment. Develop a business plan and budget before making your first purchase.

06

How can I learn more before getting started?

Visit established deer farms, attend industry conferences, join state and national deer-farm associations, and ask questions. Learning from experienced deer farmers is one of the best ways to build a successful operation.
